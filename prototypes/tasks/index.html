Design & performance plan ↗

Fewer decisions. Today, Calendar, Lists, and Settings. One reachable add button; no chat, collaboration, or decorative charts.

Nothing slips through. Overdue tasks stay above today’s plan. Tap a row to edit; complete directly; undo an accidental completion.

Keep the app light. Native implementation should keep virtualized rows, indexed date queries, and Android-scheduled reminders. This browser prototype demonstrates interactions, not measured native performance.
